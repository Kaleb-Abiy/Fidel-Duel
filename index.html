How to play
  1. Pick a topic. Each turn, the wheel lands on a letter.
  2. Say a word from the topic that starts with that letter. Your mic listens automatically.
  3. Repeats and wrong letters don't count. Keep trying until the clock runs out.
  4. Miss and you lose a life. Not in the word list? Your opponent can accept it.
